1. About this corpus

This collection contains voice recordings of all 424 sentences from the
ITA Corpus, a publicly available Japanese text corpus released in the
public domain, read aloud by Amitaro.

Format: WAV, PCM 48,000 Hz / 16-bit / Mono
        and PCM 44,100 Hz / 16-bit / Mono (both included)
Use the 44,100 Hz version for MYCOEIROINK training or combining with
dialogue voice materials.

  ITA Corpus sentence list: https://github.com/mmorise/ita-corpus

Recorded in a home recording studio. The most stable take per sentence
was selected and processed in RX10 Advanced: batch hum removal, then
manual lip and pop noise removal (taking care not to damage consonants).

2. About this style

The "Normal" style is precisely and clearly articulated, optimized for
accessibility TTS use, which tends to sound robotic when used for
character voices.

"Runrun" retains natural human imperfections — slightly softened
consonants, natural pitch variation — making it a better fit for
character dialogue. The delivery is upbeat, like sharing exciting news
with a friend.

MANA Corpus and AMITARO Corpus voice recordings are also available.

3. All available styles

"Amitaro's ITA Corpus Voice Recording" is available in the following styles.

  [All 424 ITA Corpus sentences]
  - Normal            … Precise, clearly articulated — optimized for TTS
  - Runrun (this zip) … Upbeat, bouncy delivery — like sharing good news
  - Yofukashi         … Natural, calm speech — late-night radio style
  - Punsuka           … Animated "trying-to-be-angry" character voice

  [ITA Corpus emotion 100 sentences only]
  - Sasayaki A        … Hushed whisper — heavy on breathiness
  - Sasayaki B        … Whisper with more of Amitaro's natural voice character

  Full list: https://amitaro.net/voice/corpus-list/

4. Recording environment

  Room                 Home recording studio
  Microphone           audio-technica AT4040 (condenser)
  Audio interface      Steinberg UR242
  Pop filter           STEDMAN PROSCREEN XL (metal)
  Headphones           audio-technica ATH-SX1a
  Reflection filter    CLASSIC PRO CAR900

The studio is a custom-built room (~2.4 tatami / ~3.8 m²) with
cellulose fiber insulation inside all walls, acoustic wall panels
covered with thick curtains, carpet flooring, and sound-deadening
ceiling panels.
(Measurement was taken before the curtain modification.)

Measurement data:

  A-weighted noise level:  18.5 dB  (Rion NL-52EX)
  Reverberation time T60:  220 ms

  T60 estimated by impulse integration method [1].
  Impulse response measured by TSP method [2].
  Sampling rate: 48 kHz, 24-bit.

  Measured by: Masanori Morise

Details: https://amitaro.net/about/aboutrec/
